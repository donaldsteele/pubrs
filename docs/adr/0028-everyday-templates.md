# 0028 Everyday templates (M30)

- Context: the four built-ins (flyer, trifold, business card, two-page newsletter) were built before styles, tab leaders, lists, tables, colour schemes, sheets and BorderArt existed. M30 (T22) adds the templates people reach for first and upgrades the newsletter, using only ordinary commands, deterministic ids and semantic placeholders (ADR 0006).
- Decision:
  - **List (13).** Kept as they are: `flyer`, `trifold_brochure`, `business_card`. Upgraded: `newsletter` (4 pages). New: `greeting_card`, `postcard`, `certificate`, `menu`, `letterhead`, `envelope`, `calendar`, `address_labels`, `business_cards`. `BUILTIN` is a `[&str; 13]`; the single-card `business_card` stays beside the 10-up `business_cards` sheet.
  - **Seeds and schemes.** `SeqIds` seeds 101 to 104 are unchanged; the new templates use 105 (greeting card) to 113 (business cards). Each new template, and the newsletter, starts with its own scheme (`scheme_of`): newsletter juniper_slate, greeting card plum_orchard, postcard coral_reef, certificate copper_dawn, menu saffron_market, letterhead slate_and_ember, envelope glacier_mint, calendar meadowlark, address labels orchard_frost, business cards midnight_orchid. The first three keep lantern_harbor. Colour comes from scheme slots (Main for headings and bands, Accent1 for rules, Accent2 for panels), plus white, black and the ink colour `#1F2937`. Text is never set in an accent colour, because the accents are light in several schemes; text sits in Main, ink or white (Main is dark in all twelve). A test applies another scheme to every template and checks the slot values.
  - **Styles.** Every text-heavy template calls `T::styles()`, which updates the document's default "Body", "Heading 1", "Heading 2" and "Caption" (they already exist in every new document, so `UpdateStyle` rather than `AddStyle`, whose names must be unique). Body sets `justify_limits` to the M14 defaults and `widow_orphan = 2` (TODO.md, M14 and M15); headings keep with next. Newsletter, menu, letterhead, certificate, postcard and greeting card use them; text frames take a style with `T::text_s` and override only size, alignment or colour. The flyer, trifold and business card keep direct formatting.
  - **Tables.** `T::table` wraps `DocBuilder::table` and `cell_text`, then sizes columns and rows, sets a header fill, optional banding and borders, and styles each cell's text. Every cell has text. The newsletter's two tables are named frames (`events_table`, `hours_table`) so `template_apply` returns their ids in `named_frames` and MCP clients can fill them with `table_set_cell_text`; the calendar's are `days_01` to `days_12`.
  - **Greeting card panels.** One Letter portrait page, no bleed, 18 pt margin, guides at x = 306 and y = 396. Bottom half (upright): back on the left (a credit line and a short rule), front on the right (a Main-coloured panel with the cover picture, greeting and subtitle). Top half: the inside spread, designed upright in a 612 x 396 area (inside left: picture and caption; inside right: message and signature) and then turned half a revolution about the centre of the top half, `(x, y) -> (612 - x - w, 396 - y - h)`, with every frame's `rotation` set to 180. The left inside page therefore prints top right and the right inside page top left, and all text in the top half is upside down on the sheet, as a quarter-fold card needs. The template is one printed side; users with a duplex printer can move the outside panels to the back by hand. The prompt `make_greeting_card` tells the model the upside-down text is expected.
  - **Masthead of the newsletter.** The band is inset 36 pt from the page edges (a 540 x 84 box inside the margins) and the page keeps bleed 0, rather than gaining bleed and a full-width band. The old band reached the page edge on a page with no bleed, which a desktop printer cannot print and which the checker's `object_in_unprintable_area` rule (T21) flags. An inset band needs no trim or bleed handling and prints as drawn.
  - **Printable area.** Templates with bleed 0 and no sheet (newsletter, greeting card, certificate, menu, letterhead, envelope, calendar) keep all visible content at least 18 pt inside the page. Templates with bleed (flyer, trifold, business card, postcard) or a sheet (labels, business cards sheet) are exempt. The API test applies the 18 pt rule, the 6 pt text rule and a 3:1 contrast rule (text against its frame fill, the cell fill, the frame beneath, else white) to all 13 templates, so they are clean when T21's checks arrive.
  - **Calendar.** `calendar(year)`: 12 pages, one per month, Letter landscape, margin 36, bleed 0; a picture placeholder `photo_01` to `photo_12`, a heading "January 2027" (Heading 1) and a 7 x 7 table (a header row of Sun to Sat and six week rows, so every month has the same layout). Weeks start on Sunday. Days before the 1st and after the end of the month are the neighbouring months' numbers in `#767676` (4.5:1 on white); the month's own days are bold ink. The date helpers `is_leap`, `days_in_month` and `weekday` (Sakamoto, 0 = Sunday) are in `templates.rs` with no dependency (chrono is only in `Cargo.lock` through other crates); a test checks `weekday` against an independent day count for every day from 1900 to 2100 and the leap centuries 1900, 2000 and 2100.
  - **Calendar year option.** `TemplateApply.year: Option<i32>` (serde default, `TemplateApply` now derives `Default` so literals use `..Default::default()`), validated to 1600..=9999 (`invalid_argument`), and an error for any template but `calendar` ("year applies to the calendar template only"). `templates::builtin_with(name, &TemplateOptions)` and `TemplateLibrary::load_with` carry it; `builtin(name)` is `builtin_with` with defaults. The default is `DEFAULT_CALENDAR_YEAR = 2027`, a constant, so goldens, ids and thumbnails do not depend on the clock. The GUI's New From Template passes the current UTC year (`year_of_unix_time`, pure, from `SystemTime` in `app.rs`) when `calendar` is chosen; thumbnails use the default.
  - **Sheets.** `address_labels` is the Avery 5160 label (189 x 72 pt) with `SheetFill::Sequence` and `business_cards` the 5371 card (252 x 144 pt) with `SheetFill::Repeat`, both through `sheet_preset(..).layout(..)` in the page setup, bleed 0, so the page size equals the label and imposed sheets carry no bleed. **Merge fields are deferred.** Mail merge (M24) is paused in another branch. The label is ordinary text placeholders ("Recipient name", "Street address", "City, ST 00000"); when M24 is rebased in, they become merge fields (TODO.md).
  - **Other templates.** Postcard: 432 x 288, bleed 9, margin 18, two pages (full-bleed picture and caption band; message, address block, stamp box). Certificate: Letter landscape, a rectangle frame with BorderArt `ropes` (brown, to go with its scheme), title, recipient, reason, seal picture and date and signature lines (line shapes). Menu: Letter, two pages; prices on a right tab at 460 pt with dot leaders (M16), a bulleted specials list. Letterhead: logo, name and details, rule, empty body (its placeholder hint is "Write your letter here" over an empty story, which still counts as unfilled), footer. Envelope: `Size::new(684, 297)` (#10), return address and logo top left, stamp box top right, recipient from x = 360 (centre is 342).
  - **GUI.** The New From Template dialog built the list (13 templates, two of them 4 and 12 pages) with `template_list` on every frame. The list is now built once per time the picker is open (`PubrsApp::template_list`, cleared when the dialog closes or a template is saved); thumbnails were already cached by name. No self-test step was added.
  - **Goldens.** `template_<name>_p1` for all 13 templates (the calendar shows January 2027), placeholders unfilled, test fonts only, built by one test in `render_golden.rs`.
- Consequence:
  - Unfilled picture placeholders render as nothing (as before), so the goldens show blank areas where pictures go.
  - `template_list` builds every template (the calendar is twelve tables of 49 cells), so the GUI caches it.
  - The template documents change the page ids of the newsletter (more frames, different seed use), so nothing may depend on its old ids.
