# 0019 Tab stops, leaders, bullets and numbering

- Context: T8 (M16). Paragraphs gain `tab_stops` and `list` (schema 7). A tab was only a shaped character; lists did not exist. Locked Decision 2: a paragraph with neither field and no tab character lays out exactly as in v0.1, and shaping stays one parley layout per paragraph.
- Decision:
  - **Segments are placed after shaping.** parley shapes the paragraph with every `\t` replaced by a space (same byte length, like the break characters of ADR 0015), so it breaks lines as if a tab were a space. For each line, the tabs in it split the line into segments. Segment 0 stays where parley put it; the text after tab *k* moves by `shift_k = start_k - nat_start_k`, where `nat_start` is the caret x parley gave the character after the tab and `start` is where the stop puts it. Every glyph, caret and underline after the tab moves by the shift of its segment; runs are cut at tabs so each piece carries its own shift, and the tab glyph itself is not drawn. Carets are computed before the move and shifted by segment, so hit testing across a gap picks the nearer side of the tab.
  - **Stops.** Positions are measured from the left edge of the column's text area. The stop used is the first one past the pen position (the end of the text before the tab, after earlier shifts). Left: the text starts on the stop. Center: its middle is on it. Right: its end is on it. Decimal: the first decimal separator after the tab is on it (a comma for languages that write one, else a full stop; a segment without one ends on the stop). A segment that cannot fit before its stop starts at the pen instead. With stops, the 36 pt default grid only applies past the last stop (Word's rule); with none, every tab goes to the next multiple of 36 pt.
  - **Leaders** are drawn as repeated `.`, `-` or `_` glyphs, in the font and size of the text before the tab, on a grid anchored at the column's left edge (pitch: one advance, two for dots), so the leaders of consecutive lines line up. They are glyph runs with their own text, so PDF export and text extraction need nothing new. They are not story text and have no carets.
  - **Justify.** Tab segments are never stretched: a paragraph that contains a tab is set flush left whatever its alignment, and the stops place its text. Hyphenation (ADR 0014) and drop caps and breaks (ADR 0015) work as before; a tab inside a drop cap's letters is not placed.
  - **Limit.** Lines are broken before tabs are placed, so a segment that a stop pushes past the right edge is not wrapped: it overhangs. Tabs are for short entries; long text after a tab belongs in a column or a table.
  - **List labels are generated text.** A list item's label (a bullet or a formatted number) is a glyph run on its first line, in the style of the paragraph's first character, hanging in the indent: the text starts at `max(left_indent, list.indent)` and the label at that minus `list.indent` (moved left if it would touch the text, never past the column edge). The first-line indent is ignored for list items, and a paragraph with a drop cap gets no label. Labels are not in the story: offsets, carets, find, spelling and `text_get` ranges are unaffected. `LineBox.list_label` carries the label for tests and for `text_get` (`list_label` per paragraph, additive).
  - **Counters** are a pure function of the paragraphs before an item, computed once per story in order (`list_labels` in `flow.rs`), not part of the flow state: nothing needs snapshotting for keep rewinds, and linked frames continue the count because the count is per story. One counter per level. A numbered item at level *L* continues level *L*'s count, or restarts at its `start`, and resets every deeper level; a bullet resets deeper levels too. A paragraph that is not a list item changes nothing, so the count continues across it at the same level (Word's "continue" behaviour; restart with `start`). Formats: `1.`, `a.` (then `aa.`), `A.`, `i.`, `I.` (decimal from 4000).
  - **Levels and indent.** `level` (0-8) is stored; its indent is the paragraph's `left_indent`, set by the tools and dialog to `(level + 1) * indent`. Tab at the start of a list item and Shift+Tab step the level and `left_indent` by `indent`, and move numbers that still have the default format of their level (`1.`, `a.`, `i.` repeating) to the default of the new one.
  - **`kind: none`** (not in the plan's `bullet|number`) lets a paragraph override a list its style sets, like `drop_cap.lines: 0`; `text_clear_list` writes it, and also resets `left_indent` to 0 unless `keep_indent`. `tab_stops: []` likewise clears stops a style sets.
  - **Cache.** The story's paragraphs are part of the story key, so toggling stops or lists recomposes exactly that story; counters read only earlier paragraphs of the same story. A test toggles both and compares incremental with full layout.
- Consequence:
  - Documents that contain tabs lay them out differently from v0.1 (36 pt grid instead of one space-like glyph); this is the intended change. Everything else is byte-identical (all goldens, the layout reference and the bakery LD2 test).
  - Segments pushed past the right edge overhang; justified paragraphs with tabs are flush left.
  - Labels cannot be selected or edited; to change one use the list settings.
