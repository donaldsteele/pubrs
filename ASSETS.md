## CMYK output profile (PDF/X-4)

Embedded in the release binary with `include_bytes!` (`crates/pubrs-render/src/pdf.rs`) and written
into PDF/X-4 and PDF/A-2b exports. ICC v2, class `prtr`, colour space CMYK, PCS Lab, with CMYK to Lab
and Lab to CMYK lut16 tables for the perceptual, relative colorimetric and saturation intents (237,034
bytes). It was generated on 2026-10-10 by DesignCraft's `designcraft_color::cms::icc_out::generic_cmyk_icc()`
at commit `16a7365` (storytold/designcraft, MIT OR Apache-2.0), from its parametric press model. Its
description is "DesignCraft Generic CMYK (SWOP-like)" and its copyright tag says "No copyright; made by
DesignCraft from its parametric press model. Use freely." Checked with LittleCMS 2.16 `transicc` in both
directions (CMYK 0/0/0/0 to Lab 100/0/0; Lab 50/0/0 to CMYK 40/28/31/29).

## Spelling dictionary (en_US)

Compiled into the release binary with `include_str!` (`crates/pubrs-io/src/spell.rs`, about 0.5 MB)
and used by the spell checker. It is the default-size (SCOWL size 60) American English Hunspell
dictionary, version 2026.02.25 (`hunspell-en_US-2026.02.25.zip`, SHA-256
`ac8e73310e951d88c52c2cf2ba54ceaca34f8486a81630ac8a75dc5f931179f9`), downloaded on 2026-10-11 from
the SCOWL release <https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist/releases/tag/rel-2026.02.25> (also linked from
<https://wordlist.aspell.net/dicts/>). The three files are unmodified. `README_en_US.txt` holds the
full copyright and licence text; it is compiled in as well and shown under Help → About pubrs, as
the affix file's licence asks of binary distributions.

## BorderArt (pubrs-original SVG)

Twelve decorative border patterns, each a tile (`<name>-tile.svg`, repeated along an edge) and a corner
(`<name>-corner.svg`), 32 x 32 user units. They were drawn by hand for pubrs in 2026 (M25) as plain
geometric shapes, are compiled into the binary with `include_bytes!` (`crates/pubrs-render/src/scene.rs`),
and are released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication). `assets/borderart/LICENSE` holds the
legal code, downloaded from <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.txt> on 2026-10-11.
The patterns are: stars, hearts, dots, waves, zigzag, diamonds, leaves, ropes, checks, scallops, arrows
and snowflakes. They contain no Microsoft or Adobe artwork.
